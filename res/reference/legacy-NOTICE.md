[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

SHA-256 hashes to the frozen [seed allowlist](legacy-manifest.json).

## Adaptations and evidence limits

The frozen seed metadata records historical bilingual HTML and extracted
figure files. The translator, generation method, figure extraction history
and human review record are unknown. This source snapshot omits those
adaptations and includes the original PDFs. Upload them through the normal
PDF workflow to read or translate them.

The repository's AGPL-3.0-only license covers the project's original code.
The underlying paper material retains its separate CC BY 4.0 terms.
Keep this notice and the CC BY 4.0 license with copies of the original PDFs.
